## Aesthetic Deep-Green Dark Mode Design
1. **Background Palette**:
   - Deep Obsidian-Emerald mesh gradient: `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, #0a2621 0%, #051814 50%, #020c0a 100%)`
   - Primary dark surface: `#041411`
   - Secondary card surface: `#08221c`
   - Frosted Glass cards: `rgba(8, 34, 28, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`
2. **Borders & Glows**:
   - Subtle emerald borders: `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.16)`
   - Glowing hover state: `0 0 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.22)`
3. **Typography & Contrast**:
   - Primary text: `#f2fbf7` (maximum legibility without harsh white glare)
   - Secondary text: `#8fa89f` (gentle sage tint)
   - Accent gradients: Antigravity Emerald & Cyan gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #059669 0%, #10b981 40%, #0d9488 75%, #0284c7 100%)`)
